UPLOAD TO INTERNET (FREE)
-------------------------
1. Unzip this folder
2. Go to https://app.netlify.com
3. Sign up (free)
4. Click "Add new site" → "Deploy manually"
5. Drag ALL files (index.html, styles.css, main.js) into the box
6. Wait 1 minute — you get a link like https://something.netlify.app

UPLOAD TO CPANEL / HOSTING
--------------------------
1. Unzip
2. Upload index.html, styles.css, main.js to public_html
3. Visit your domain in Chrome / Safari

OPEN ON YOUR PC (WITHOUT HOSTING)
---------------------------------
1. Unzip
2. Double-click index.html
   (Charts need internet for fonts/CDN)

CHANGE PASSWORD
